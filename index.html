Daily Anti-Inflammatory Protocol — Every Single Day
Waterson: "I encourage every actor to have daily shots of turmeric or ginger." This is not optional.
① Turmeric Tonic (Sarah Sugden's recipe)
6 pcs Fresh Turmeric root (each ≈2cm) — Billa Bio
3 pcs Fresh Ginger root — Billa Bio
2 Lemons or Oranges (juice only)
2 tbsp Manuka or raw Honey — dm Bio
3 pinches Black Pepper — MANDATORY for absorption
Method: Don't peel roots — scrub clean, keep skin on. Blend everything in high-speed blender until smooth. Strain through a fine sieve. Take as a shot straight up, OR add ¾ tbsp to hot water for tea, OR add to sparkling water with ice and mint. Batch-make on Sunday for the whole week — stores in a sealed jar in the fridge for 7 days.

③ Lemon + Ginger Shot
Juice of ½ lemon + 5g grated fresh ginger in 30ml warm water. Take with or after the turmeric tonic. Immune system boost — daily protocol.

A major Consumer Reports investigation (Oct 2025) tested 23 popular protein powders and found over two-thirds contained dangerous levels of lead — some by more than 10× the safe daily limit. Plant-based powders (pea, rice, hemp) averaged 9× more lead than whey. BioTechUSA has not been independently certified for heavy metals.

What to buy instead:
